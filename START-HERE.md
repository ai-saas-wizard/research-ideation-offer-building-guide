# Start here

This folder is your workspace for **Research, Ideation & Offer-Building Guide**. Your AI assistant uses it to guide you through each lesson, save your work in the right place, and carry each lesson's result into the next one.

## What you need

- **Claude Code** (in the Claude desktop app or a terminal) or **Codex**
- This folder, saved anywhere on your computer. You can rename it.

## Start the course

1. Open this folder in Claude Code or Codex.
2. Type: **Let's start the course.**
3. Your assistant creates your lesson folders and a progress file, then starts Lesson 01.

**Coming back later?** Open the same folder and type **Let's continue.** Your assistant checks your progress file and picks up where you stopped.

**Start a new chat for each lesson.** When a lesson ends, your assistant will remind you: start a new chat with this same folder open and type **Let's continue.** Nothing is lost, because every lesson reads your earlier work from this folder. Long chats get summarised automatically and can lose details, so if a chat gets very long in the middle of a lesson, start a new one the same way.

If your assistant doesn't seem to know about the course, type: **Read AGENTS.md and follow it.**

## Your part

- **Answer honestly.** Rough notes and rambling are fine; your assistant organizes them. You can skip any question.
- **Read every document your assistant writes, and correct anything that isn't accurate.** This is the most important step. Later lessons build on these files, so a wrong detail carries forward.
- **Lesson 02 pauses on purpose.** Your assistant shows you a research brief and waits. Read it, correct it, then approve it by typing: *I approve this research brief. Conduct the research and create R02-market-research-report.md.*
- **Lessons 03, 04 and 06 use your screenshots.** Save them into that lesson's `screenshots` folder, then tell your assistant they're there.
- **Lesson 06 has numbers.** Your assistant shows every calculation. Check that the hours, costs and prices are yours. A price in your plan is something to try, not proof that anyone will buy.
- **Lesson 07 has two parts.** First you plan a small test, and your file stays marked *test pending*. After you have actually shown your offer to real people, come back with your dated notes and your assistant adds the results.
- **Do Lesson 08 before anyone pays you.** It turns everything you plan to sell into a checklist of what may apply where you live and sell (registering a business, tax, licences or certificates for your kind of work, insurance, terms, privacy), with where to check each item. It is not legal advice: for anything unclear, ask an accountant, a lawyer, or the official body it names.
- **Keep files where they are.** Don't rename or move the R-files; later lessons look for them in their folders.
- **Web search helps in Lessons 02–04 and 08.** If your assistant can't search the web, it gives you a search plan to follow yourself instead.

## The lessons

| Lesson | The question it answers | What you end up with |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | What can I credibly help with? | Capability brief (R01) |
| 02 | Which markets merit investigation? | Research brief and market research report (R02) |
| 03 | What alternatives and buying paths exist? | Competitor and funnel comparison (R03) |
| 04 | Which person and problem should I focus on? | Ideal customer profile (R04) |
| 05 | What progress can I help them make, and where are the limits? | Outcome and boundary brief (R05) |
| 06 | What will I offer, how much work will it take, and what prices should I try? | Offer plan (R06) |
| 07 | How will I show, test, and improve the complete offer? | Offer map and test log (R07) |
| 08 | What do I need to sort out before anyone pays me? | Legal and setup checklist (R08) |

If you receive a newer version of this kit, put it in this folder and ask your assistant to update your course. If you got the course from GitHub, just ask your assistant to update your course. Either way, your work stays as it is.
